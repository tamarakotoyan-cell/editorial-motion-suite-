# Delivery QA checklist

## Contract

- Duration and frame count match the contract.
- Width, height, frame rate, codec, pixel format and audio layout match the intended platform.
- Every required variant exists and is clearly named.

## Editorial

- Headline, statistic, commissioner, source and CTA match approved copy.
- No claim is stronger than the source.
- Percentage denominator and base are visible or unambiguous.
- Research source line is readable at delivery size.

## Picture and motion

- Opening, proof and ending each have one focal point.
- Text stays inside format-safe areas and is legible at thumbnail size.
- Crops do not remove faces, devices or evidence required by the beat.
- Persistent objects do not jump between adjacent frames.
- The payoff begins after any covering layer has cleared.
- No unintended flash, blank frame, duplicated frame, tear, watermark or missing asset.
- Final dead tail is measured and intentional.

## Sound

- Picture communicates when muted.
- Hero transient aligns with the intended frame.
- Minor moves may remain silent; do not fail a render for selective silence.
- No clipping, clicks, truncated tails or unexplained noise floor.

## Accessibility

- Contrast and type size are sufficient at target dimensions.
- Captions do not cover proof or source lines.
- Reduced-motion output preserves sequence and comparison.
- No rapid alternating high-contrast flashes.

## Package

- Editable source, assets, font notes and rights log are present.
- Generated derivatives are separate from originals.
- Delivery report distinguishes tested results from human approval.
